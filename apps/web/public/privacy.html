Privacy Policy for New Me

New Me ("the app") is a habit-tracking application. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to register. Passwords are hashed (bcrypt) and never stored in plain text.
  • Habit data: the habits you create, their names, notes, schedules, and your daily completion history.
  • Notification preferences: reminder times and rules you configure. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; we do not use a third-party push notification service.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the app's core functionality: authenticating you, storing and displaying your habits, and scheduling reminders you've asked for. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by our infrastructure provider (NeonDB) and served via an API hosted on Render. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Retention and Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (habits, history, reminders) at any time from the app's Profile screen via the "Delete Account" option. This action is immediate and irreversible.

Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs at this time.

Children's Privacy

New Me is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
